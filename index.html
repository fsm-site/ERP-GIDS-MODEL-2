Frequently asked

How long does a screen replacement take?

Most iPhone screens are replaced in about 60 minutes if the part is in stock. Older or rarer models may take a day.

What does the warranty cover?

Repairs carry 7 days of cover on the part and workmanship. Refurbished phones are covered for 30 days. Physical damage and liquid exposure after the repair are not included.

Will I lose my data?

Hardware repairs do not touch your storage, but we always recommend backing up before you hand the device over.

Do you buy or trade in old phones?

Yes. Bring the unit to any branch for a free assessment and we will quote a trade-in value on the spot.
